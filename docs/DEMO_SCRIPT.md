## Filming notes

- The purpose sentence, verbatim (also on /about and in `lib/config.ts`):
  > "CrestWatch turns the government's river forecasts — written for engineers — into one plain sentence about your own house: when the water is coming, how high, and whether it reaches your door, in a district that once stayed flooded for six months while the forecasts sat unread."
- The 0:45 shot needs a LIKELY/BORDERLINE spread between a house and a
  road. If the rivers are quiet on filming day, use the replay screen for
  drama and film the live screens as-is — honesty over theater.
- The video must state: names, app name, what it does, who it's for, and
  the tools/languages used (Next.js, TypeScript, USGS/NOAA/Census APIs).
