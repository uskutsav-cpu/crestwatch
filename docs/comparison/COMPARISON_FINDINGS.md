## Advantages of the official baseline that must remain visible

NOAA is the authoritative forecast publisher. Its page supplies issue-time context, alert status, flow information, flood categories, reliability context, historical crest lists and links to additional official resources. CrestWatch relies on those public sources and is not a substitute for emergency instructions. A fair comparison allows participants to use relevant official help and map resources; it must not deliberately restrict the baseline to a cropped screenshot or hide its controls.

## Hypotheses to test, not findings

1. Grouping the observation, forecast high, flood stage and time may improve lookup completion.
2. Connecting an explored level to an official statement may reduce false claims of current road closures.
3. Explicit gauge/property limitations may reduce false property all-clears.

Task selection focuses on CrestWatch's intended interpretation problem. Results cannot establish superiority over NOAA for professional hydrology, alerts, inundation mapping or every river-data task.
