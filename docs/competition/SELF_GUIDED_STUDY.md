# Self-guided comparison

Share https://crestwatch-river-guide.utsavsresearch.chatgpt.site/test with adult volunteers. They need no account or researcher present. Ask people to take part once without coaching. About 10–15 minutes, three tasks per interface. Interface order is randomized per anonymous session; equal group sizes are not guaranteed.

Responses save centrally in the Site's private D1 database. Use Sites Settings → database viewer or the Sites database tools to read participant_sessions and participant_attempts. This is separate from the facilitator's browser-local /review tool. There is deliberately no public results listing or publicly shared spreadsheet.

Join attempts on session_id = sessions.id. Keep all rows, including unfinished sessions and skipped, technical-problem, assisted and timed-out attempts. next_index=6 with finished_at means submitted all tasks, not correct answers. No record means no evidence. Score raw explanations using the existing task rubric before making any success or understanding claims. Do not compare self-declared “ready” as verified correct.

Times measure from Start to the participant's stop choice, capped at 120 seconds, including page opening; answer typing is excluded. Delayed returns are classified time_limit. Device category is inferred from viewport. Help and familiarity are self-reported. Cookie-based sessions cannot prove unique people or prevent repeat participation. Repeated tasks may produce learning effects; randomized order reduces, but does not eliminate them. Volunteers are adults who opt in, not a representative sample. Live readings may change between tasks.

No names, email addresses or property addresses are requested. Responses are private to the project; hosting infrastructure may process request metadata. The participant can resume or delete their own response in the same browser while its seven-day cookie remains. Deleting cookies does not delete stored responses. Researcher should review and remove accidentally submitted personal details using authorized database management tools, and delete study records when no longer needed. Never publish raw private answers without reviewing for identifiers.

The software QA records used during development are explicitly labelled SOFTWARE QA and withdrawn after verification. They are not participant evidence.
