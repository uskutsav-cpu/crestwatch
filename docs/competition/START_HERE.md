# CrestWatch | submission and pilot kit
Prepared October 25, 2026. Materials are ready to use; no human study, expert review, video recording, or competition submission has been completed by preparing them.

## Time-sensitive priorities
The national 2026 rulebook lists Monday, October 26 at noon EDT, which is 11:00 a.m. CDT (America/Chicago). Verify the live submission portal and your participating district's instructions. Some district pages conflict; do not assume a later deadline. Complete the submission before the applicable deadline.

1. Confirm registration, district eligibility, team details and the portal's required fields yourself. Do not put private registration information in public project documents.
2. Complete the factual ownership/disclosure worksheet. The official rules permit automated support but require full disclosure and significant student contributions and technical understanding. This project received substantial automated coding assistance. The worksheet does not establish eligibility; it records evidence accurately.
3. Rehearse and record the 2:45 demo. Include every participant's name, purpose, audience, tools/languages and working interactions. Leave time to upload and check public viewing.
4. If time permits, run a small real pilot using USER_TESTING.md and the browser-local Review Lab. Two thoughtful sessions are more useful than invented or rushed results. Report the exact sample; a pilot is not proof of effectiveness.
5. Review the application drafts, replace all factual blanks yourself, check character limits in the actual portal, and submit. Save the confirmation and the exact source archive privately.
